Matrices

The vertex shader transforms the star vertices through the following sequence of operations: scaling, rotation, and translation. Scale and rotation are applied via 2x2 transformation matrices:

f(x,y)= [cos⁡(45°)−sin⁡(45°)sin⁡(45°)cos⁡(45°)] [0.5000.5][xy]+[0.2−0.1] f(x, y) = \begin{bmatrix} \cos(45\degree) & -\sin(45\degree) \\ \sin(45\degree) & \cos(45\degree) \end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix} 0.5 & 0 \\ 0 & 0.5 \end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix} x \\ y \end{bmatrix} + \begin{bmatrix} 0.2 \\ -0.1 \end{bmatrix}
